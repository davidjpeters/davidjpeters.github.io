Why Does This Matter?

The reason you should care about the Optimal Power Flow problem, is that it
directly impacts the reliability and cost of the power we use. In addition,
renewable energy is unreliable and often reliant on external factors, meaning
managing power more difficult. By optimizing the way power flows across the
grid, OPF keeps electricity affordable, reduces environmental impact, and
ensures that lights stay on, even as energy systems evolve.

Money

The more efficient the grid, the less we pay for power.

Renewable Energy

As it stands, regardless of how much access we have to renewable energy, we don't
have the infrastructure to use it reliably. The better we can optimize our
electrical systems, the more we can utilize new renewable energy sources.
